# Code of conduct

## The short version

Be decent to the people you are talking to. This is a small project that people work on in
their spare time.

## What is expected

- Assume the other person is trying to help, even when their message is short or their English
  is not fluent.
- Criticise the code, the idea, or the decision — never the person.
- Accept that a maintainer may say no. A project that accepts everything becomes unmaintainable.
- Respect that other people run other operating systems and other hardware than you do.

## What is not accepted

- Insults, harassment, or unwelcome personal attention.
- Comments that target someone's identity: origin, gender, sexuality, religion, disability, age.
- Publishing someone else's private information.
- Deliberately wasting the time of contributors or maintainers.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness behaviour that breaks these rules, contact the repository owner
through a [private security advisory](https://github.com/hjrb/BootManager/security/advisories/new),
which is the only private channel this repository has. Reports are handled confidentially.

Maintainers may edit, hide, or delete comments, close issues and pull requests, and block
accounts that do not follow these rules.

This document is a short, project-specific summary. For a fuller treatment of the same ideas,
see the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/).
